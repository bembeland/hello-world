🌊 Fourier, in one screen

  • Denoise: low-pass Fourier (k≤2) smooths GW1–11 — gold line. Residuals = actual − smoothed (chart below).
  • Project: ridge-regularised seasonal wave (38/19 GW) → GW12–38 between 1.37–1.50. Naive unregularised fit explodes to xG≈35 on 11 points — rejected.
  • Rule: Fourier = smooth cycles only. Red cards, VAR, injuries, weather, congestion = match events (commentary section), added as covariates, never as sine waves.

💡 In short

  • Most weeks sit near 1.41 xG/team — tails (GW6, GW8) make headlines, not trends.
  • League Scored = Conceded, always. Clubs move along the curve; the league mirror doesn't.
  • Projections are distributions, not scores: expect most weeks inside the pink band.
  • Finishing streaks (Chelsea +6.3 over xG, Forest −7.9 after GW11) usually regress.

🧪 Method & sources (short)

  • Actuals: 110 scorelines, FBref (303 goals). GW xG est. = goals × 1.025 (calibrated to Squawka/Opta season xG 310.69; GW11 check −5.8%). FBref lost its Opta feed Jan 2026; Understat offline since 2 Nov 2026 — hence the labelled estimate.
  • Models: (1) Normal projection μ=1.41, σ=0.26, widening bands. (2) Fourier low-pass + ridge seasonal wave. (3) Binary logistic regression (one-vs-rest W, D, L + Over 2.5 + BTTS), gradient-descent fit on the 110 matches using only pre-match (prior-week) strengths; probabilities shown per club-week, accuracy reported in the club panel. Poisson still gives likely score & xPts.
  • Backtest: expanding window, prior weeks only, 4-match prior shrinkage. Future predictions get each club's backtest-bias correction (×0.85–1.18, shrunk 50%).
  • Sources: FBref fixtures · Squawka/Opta xG · BBC table · BBC live text · Guardian MBM · Footly calendar · NBC fixtures · Kaggle Football Events
  • Built 26 Nov 2026 (America/Toronto). Estimates ±~0.2 xG. Not betting advice.
